┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           BEFORE vs AFTER                                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

BEFORE (Time-Based):
  Caption: "Ayush, so what do you think about this whole situation here"
  Duration: 3.5 seconds
  Words: 12
  Issues: Too long, wraps, hard to style
  
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Ayush, so what do you think about this      │
  │ whole situation here                        │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

AFTER (Word-Aware):
  Caption 1: "Ayush, so what do"        [1.2s] 
  Caption 2: "you think about this"     [1.1s]
  Caption 3: "whole situation here"     [1.2s]
  
  Benefits:
  ✅ Short, readable (3-5 words)
  ✅ Precise timing (word-level)
  ✅ Easy to style (fits any screen)
  ✅ Keyword highlighting works
  ✅ Professional appearance
